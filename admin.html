Collections

A collection appears on the homepage once a listing is assigned to it. The footer collection links are fixed in the page header, so a brand-new collection will show in the homepage tiles and sections, not in the footer, until the footer is updated.

3. Photos, videos & colour per photo

Pick a colour for each photo. On the live product page, choosing that colour switches the main photo to the first photo of that colour. Leave a photo on “All colours” if it is a group or detail shot that suits every colour. Drag order: use ↑ ↓ to move a photo, ★ to make it the main photo.

Videos

Videos under about 4 MB can be uploaded here. For a larger video, upload it elsewhere and paste the link (MP4, WebM, or a YouTube link all play on the product page).

4. Size chart

Upload the size chart image for this listing, or paste its URL. It shows as its own “Size Chart” section on the product page, with a link to open it full size.
