Shipping Policy

This page mirrors the Shipping Policy from magracia.com. Purchases on this site are completed on Etsy, where the Etsy checkout terms also apply.

At Magracia, we're committed to getting your order to you as quickly and safely as possible.

Domestic Shipping (Pakistan)

Orders within Pakistan are processed and delivered within 5–7 business days from the date of purchase.

International Shipping (US & UAE)

We currently ship to the United States and United Arab Emirates. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days, depending on customs processing and your location.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days before dispatch. You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking details once your order ships.

Shipping Costs

Free shipping for domestic orders (Pakistan). Paid Shipping for International orders (UAE/USA).

Standard Shipping

5–14 business days. Tracking Number Provided. Delivery times may vary depending on destination and customs clearance.

Customs & Duties (International Orders)

UK & EU: the seller will cover applicable Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) charges including import duties and customs fees where applicable; buyers are responsible for VAT / sales tax collected by Etsy at checkout.

USA: buyers are responsible for any applicable DDP-related import duties, tariffs, and customs charges; any VAT/sales tax collected by Etsy is charged at checkout.

Other international destinations: buyers are responsible for customs duties, import taxes, VAT, sales taxes and other import-related charges, unless otherwise stated.

Delays

While we aim to meet the delivery windows above, delays may occasionally occur due to customs, weather, or courier issues beyond our control. We appreciate your patience.

Returns

Items are eligible for return within 14 days of delivery. Please see our Refund Policy for full details.
